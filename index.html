How these are built

Each course is generated from its own primary sources — lecture slides, transcripts, the syllabus, assigned textbook chapters and papers — and sequenced so that every term is defined before it is used. Equations carry numbers and are cross-referenced from a per-course formula sheet. Where a source disagrees with the literature, the page follows the course for anything assessed and flags the discrepancy in place rather than propagating it silently.

The pages are static HTML with no external dependencies: they render identically offline, in either theme, on a phone or a desktop.
